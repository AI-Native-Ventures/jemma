Helper instructions

Making fake handwritten test papers so we can test which AI reads handwriting best

Made-up only  Never use a real child's name or a real child's paper.

Fill in the papers (about 10 in total)

  1. Take 3 or 4 printed tests each. No printer? Write the question numbers 1 to 10 down a plain page and answer beside them.
  2. Write a made-up name at the top of each paper. Use a different name for every paper (list below).
  3. Answer like a Grade 6 learner would. Get most questions right, and make 2 to 4 mistakes per paper using the "Mistakes to plant" column on the memo.
  4. Make the papers different: one almost all right, one with lots of mistakes, some neat, some messy, some in pencil, some with crossings-out. Show working on questions 4, 5 and 10.
  5. Mark one paper with a hard-to-read name, and write one answer very messily.

Photograph them

  1. One photo per paper with a phone. Mix it up: most flat in good light, a few at a slight angle, one in dim light, one with a shadow across it.
  2. Also photograph the memo page once.
