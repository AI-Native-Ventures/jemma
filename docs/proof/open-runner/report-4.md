## Mistake (gap 1)

**Label:** Adds the tops and the bottoms  
**Count:** 8 wrong answers  
**Example:** 1/2 + 1/4 = 2/6

Learners add the top numbers together and the bottom numbers together because they treat each fraction as two whole numbers sitting side by side.

## Missing basic skill

Making equivalent fractions so both have the same denominator.

## Explanation (teacher can say aloud)

1. The bottom number shows how many equal parts the whole is cut into, so it stays the same when you add.
2. First change both fractions so they have the same bottom number, then add only the tops.

## Reteach (whole group: Amina, Thabo, Naledi)

**Title:** Adding fractions with different denominators  
**Audience:** Amina, Thabo, Naledi  
**Minutes:** 10  
**You need:** Fraction strips or paper strips, pencils, paper

**Steps:**
1. (3 minutes) Show 1/2 and 1/4 on fraction strips. Ask how many parts each whole has.
2. (3 minutes) Cut the half into quarters so both fractions show four parts. Then add the tops: 2/4 + 1/4 = 3/4.
3. (4 minutes) Learners practise 2/3 + 1/6 and 1 1/2 + 2 1/4 with strips, changing to the same bottom number first.

**Exit question:** What is 1/2 + 1/4?

**Correct answers from the memo:** Q1 = 3/4, Q2 = 5/6, Q4 = 3 3/4
