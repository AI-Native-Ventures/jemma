GRADE 4 MATHEMATICS

SCHEME OF WORK · YEAR 2026 · KENYA · THREE TERMS

School year: 2026. This school year has three terms. The scheme shows the sub-strands taught each week, the learning experiences and the way the teacher checks learning.

Strands covered this year:

  • Numbers
  • Measurement and money
  • Geometry, patterns and data

Term 1 has 13 weeks. Term 2 has 13 weeks. Term 3 has 10 weeks. Teachers follow the order of the sub-strands. A sub-strand may be taught again in a later term when the scheme says so.

An invented scheme of work for testing a reader. Not copied from any ministry document.

TERM 1 · 13 weeks

WeeksSub-strandLearning experiencesAssessment
1 to 3Whole numbers up to 10 000Learners read, write and order numbers up to 10 000 using bundles of sticks and a place value chart, and round to the nearest ten and hundred.Oral questions
4 to 6Addition and subtractionLearners add and subtract numbers up to 10 000 with and without regrouping, and solve story problems about the market and the school.Written exercise
7 to 10Multiplication tablesLearners build the tables of 2 to 10 with counters and arrays, chant them, and use them to solve problems about equal groups.Tables quiz
11 to 13Length in metres and centimetresLearners measure the classroom and the playground with a metre rule and a tape, and change between metres and centimetres.Practical task

TERM 2 · 13 weeks

WeeksSub-strandLearning experiencesAssessment
1 to 4DivisionLearners share objects equally into groups, write division sentences and link division to the multiplication tables.Written exercise
5 to 7Fractions: halves, quarters and eighthsLearners fold paper and cut fruit to show halves, quarters and eighths, and write them as fractions.Practical task
8 to 9Decimals: tenths and hundredthsGrade 6 content, shown for the teacher only. Do not teach this in Grade 4. It is listed here because the school's old scheme had it.None
10 to 11MoneyLearners use play money in shillings to buy and sell, add prices and work out change.Role play and checklist
12 to 13TimeLearners read the time to the hour, half hour and quarter hour, and say how many days are in a week and months in a year.Oral questions

TERM 3 · 10 weeks

WeeksSub-strandLearning experiencesAssessment
1 to 2Length in metres and centimetresTaught again, with perimeter. Learners measure the sides of a desk and a garden plot and add them up.Practical task
3 to 4Shapes: triangles, squares and circlesLearners find shapes in the classroom, name them and count their sides and corners.Checklist
5 to 6Angles and turnsLearners turn a pointer a quarter and a half turn, and find square corners in objects around them.Observation
7 to 8Number patternsLearners continue patterns of numbers and beads, and say the rule of each pattern.Written exercise
9 to 10Data: tally charts and pictogramsLearners collect data on favourite fruits, make a tally chart and a pictogram, and answer questions about it.End of year test
Length in metres and centimetres is taught in Term 1 and again in Term 3. Decimals (tenths and hundredths) is Grade 6 content.
