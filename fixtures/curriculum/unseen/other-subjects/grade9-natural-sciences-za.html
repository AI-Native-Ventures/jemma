Annual Teaching Plan 2026

Grade 9 · Natural Sciences · South Africa · Senior Phase
School year: 2026Terms: 4Contact time: 3.5 hours a week

About this plan

This plan shows what is taught in each of the four school terms and the practical work or assessment that goes with it. Teachers follow the order of the topics. A topic may take more than one week. The weeks shown are the teaching weeks of the term, not the calendar weeks.

Strands

  • Life and Living: cells, plants, ecosystems and the way people change the environment.
  • Matter and Materials: atoms, elements, reactions, acids and bases.
  • Energy and Change: electricity, heat and forces.
  • Planet Earth and Beyond: rocks, weather and the solar system.

How to read the tables

Each term has one table. The first column gives the weeks, the second the topic, the third what learners do and learn, and the fourth the practical work or assessment task. Topics marked as belonging to another grade are shown for revision only and are not assessed in Grade 9.

Term 1 · 10 weeks

WeeksTopicContent and conceptsPractical work or assessment
1 to 3Cells and their partsPlant and animal cells, the nucleus, cell membrane, cytoplasm and chloroplasts. Using a microscope. Why cells divide.Make and draw a slide of onion skin. Labelled diagram test.
4 to 5Photosynthesis and respirationHow green plants make food. How all living things release energy from food. Word equations for both.Test a leaf for starch. Investigation report.
6States of matter and the particle modelSolids, liquids and gases. Melting and boiling. This topic belongs to Grade 8 and is shown for revision only. It is not assessed in Grade 9.No assessment.
7 to 8Atoms, elements and the periodic tableProtons, neutrons and electrons. Reading the periodic table. Metals and non-metals.Build atom models. Short test.
9 to 10Chemical reactions and word equationsSigns of a reaction. Reactants and products. Conservation of mass. Writing word equations.Term 1 test (50 marks).

Term 2 · 9 weeks

WeeksTopicContent and conceptsPractical work or assessment
1 to 2Acids, bases and the pH scaleProperties of acids and bases. Indicators. The pH scale. Neutralisation in daily life.Test household liquids with red cabbage indicator.
3 to 4Reproduction in flowering plantsParts of a flower. Pollination and fertilisation. Seeds and fruits. How seeds spread.Dissect a flower and draw it. Practical report.
5 to 6Electric circuitsCurrent, voltage and resistance. Circuit symbols. Measuring with an ammeter and a voltmeter.Build a simple circuit and record readings.
7 to 9Series and parallel circuitsComparing the two kinds of circuit. Brightness of bulbs. Why homes use parallel wiring.Mid-year examination (80 marks).

Term 3 · 10 weeks

WeeksTopicContent and conceptsPractical work or assessment
1 to 2Electric circuitsContinued from Term 2: current, voltage and resistance. Practice problems and a second practical.Circuit problems worksheet.

6 to 8Rocks and the rock cycleIgneous, sedimentary and metamorphic rocks. Weathering and erosion. How rocks change over time.Identify a rock collection. Practical test.
9 to 10Weather and climateAir pressure, wind and clouds. Reading a weather map. The difference between weather and climate.Term 3 test (50 marks).

Term 4 · 8 weeks

WeeksTopicContent and conceptsPractical work or assessment
1 to 2Ecosystems and food websProducers, consumers and decomposers. Food chains and food webs. How energy flows.Draw a food web for a local habitat.

Notes for teachers

  • Electric circuits is taught over two terms: the start is in Term 2 and the practice is in Term 3.
  • States of matter and the particle model is a Grade 8 topic. It appears once in Term 1 as revision.
  • Safety: learners wear eye protection for all practical work with acids and heat.
