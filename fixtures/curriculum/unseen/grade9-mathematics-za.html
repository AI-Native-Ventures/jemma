Annual Teaching Plan 2026

Grade 9 · Mathematics · South Africa · Senior Phase
School year: 2026Terms: 4Contact time: 4.5 hours a week

About this plan

This plan shows what is taught in each of the four school terms and the task or assessment that goes with it. Teachers follow the order of the topics. A topic may take more than one week. The weeks shown are the teaching weeks of the term, not the calendar weeks.

Content areas

  • Numbers and operations: integers, exponents, rational and irrational numbers, ratio and rate, and money.
  • Algebra and functions: expressions, equations, patterns and graphs.
  • Space and shape: angles, triangles, congruence and Pythagoras.
  • Measurement: surface area and volume.
  • Data and chance: summarising data and probability.

How to read the tables

Each term has one table. The first column gives the weeks, the second the topic, the third what learners do and learn, and the fourth the task or assessment. Topics marked as belonging to another grade are shown for information only and are not assessed in Grade 9.

Notes for teachers

  • Ratio, rate and proportion is taught over two terms: the start is in Term 2 and the practice is in Term 3.
  • Trigonometry (sine, cosine and tangent) is a Grade 10 topic. It appears once in Term 4 for information only.
  • Calculators are used from Term 1. Learners show their working in every task.
