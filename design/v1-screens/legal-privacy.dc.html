Privacy

Your child's work stays yours. Here is what Jemma keeps and who sees it.

What Jemma keeps

  • Paper photos and marks
  • Lessons and the answers to them
  • Teacher notes and attendance
  • Activities you add

Who sees what

  • TeacherLearners in their own class
  • ParentChildren they are linked to, after the teacher says yes
  • ChildOnly themselves
  • Jemma teamNobody, unless you ask us for help

AI services

Jemma sends paper photos to an AI service to read them. That service doesn't keep them or learn from them.

Children

South Africa's POPIA law treats under-18s as children, so a parent or guardian agrees before a child's work is used.

Your rights

You can see, fix, download or delete what Jemma keeps.
