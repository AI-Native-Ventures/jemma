Why we can't add the bottoms

Jemma, for Amina
1:123:04
EN

Read along

Lots of people write 2/6 for a half plus a quarter. It feels right: one and one make two, two and four make six.

But the bottom number isn't something you add. It tells you the size of the pieces.

Halves are big pieces. Quarters are smaller. Before we add, we make the pieces the same size.
