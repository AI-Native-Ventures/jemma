Fraction strips · 10 minutes · whole class

10 min

You need: 3 paper strips per learner, all the same length.

  1. 2 minFold one strip into halves and one into quarters.
  2. 3 minLay them side by side. How many quarters fit in a half? Write 1/2 = 2/4.
  3. 3 minAdd 1/2 + 1/4 with the strips. Count the quarters: 3/4. Ask why 2/6 is smaller than 1/2.
  4. 2 minExit question on the board: 1/3 + 1/6.
